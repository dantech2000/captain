# Feature 0007: Logs

- Milestone: M3 (detail), a second pass on the Logs tab
- Status: In progress
- Design: the Logs tab of the v2 inspector, with a two-row toolbar

## Goal

Make the Logs tab good enough to debug a container without the Docker CLI: search, times, a follow mode that stays out of the way, and copy.

## In scope

- Timestamps: Captain asks Docker for timestamps and strips the RFC 3339 prefix from each line. A time column shows the local time as `HH:MM:SS`. The Time chip hides and shows it.
- Search: a search field filters lines by a case-insensitive substring, together with the level filter. Each match in a line gets a bold, highlighted background. The toolbar shows "n of m lines".
- Follow: the list sticks to the newest line. A scroll up pauses following and shows a "Jump to latest ↓ (n new)" pill. The pill, a scroll back to the end, or the Following / Paused button resumes it.
- Copy: the Copy button copies the lines that pass the filters, with their times when the time column shows. The copy icon on a hovered line, or a right-click on the line, copies that line.
- Clear: the Clear button empties the view. The container keeps its logs.
- Streams: stderr text has a light red tint, on top of the level color.

## Out of scope

- Regular expressions and fuzzy search.
- Loading lines older than the last 500.
- Saving logs to a file.
- Logs of several containers or of a Compose project in one view.
- Wrapping long lines.

## Notes

- The local time zone comes from `date +%z` once per run. A daylight saving change during a run shows after a restart. Where `date` is missing (Windows), times show in UTC.
- The view keeps the last 2,000 lines. Older lines drop off the top, also while following is paused.

## Verification

1. `docker run -d --name captain-logs busybox sh -c 'while true; do date; echo oops >&2; sleep 1; done'` shows a new line every second with a time column. The `oops` lines have a red tint.
2. The Time chip hides and shows the time column.
3. Typing `OOPS` in the search field shows only the `oops` lines, with `oops` highlighted, and the count changes to "n of m lines".
4. A scroll up shows "Paused" and the jump pill, and its count goes up each second. The pill scrolls to the end and shows "Following" again.
5. Copy puts the visible lines on the clipboard. A right-click on one line copies only that line.
6. Clear empties the view. New lines appear again, and `docker logs captain-logs` still shows the old ones.
7. `cargo test -p captain-docker --test live_logs -- --ignored` passes.
